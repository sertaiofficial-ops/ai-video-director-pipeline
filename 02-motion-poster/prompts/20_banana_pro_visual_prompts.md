# Banana Pro — 20 Premium Poster Visual Prompts

## Shared quality lock

All prompts below are written in natural language for Banana Pro. In every generation, keep the **original fictional subject**, **premium cinematic poster still**, **vertical 9:16** and **no readable text, no logos, no watermark** parts. The poster feel should come from a strong silhouette, controlled negative space, material quality, lighting hierarchy and a single focal point, not from typography. In character images, keep face, hair, skin, marks, clothing and accessories as identity anchors; when regenerating the same character, change only pose, gaze, lens or a single action.

**Shared negative block:** no readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no brand marks, no signature, no border, no collage, no split screen, no duplicate subject, no extra limbs, no extra fingers, no malformed hands, no plastic skin, no muddy details, no generic face, no random costume changes, no background crowd, no visible UI.

## Shared usage note

If one of the characters is taken into video, use the generated image as the **starting frame** and repeat the same identity anchor in the video prompt. In object or environment images, keep camera movement on a single axis; do not change the object's form or the environment's geometry. Adding the poster's title and brand text in the final design layer, instead of having the generation model draw it, preserves visual continuity and legibility.
