# Google Flow Lab — 20 Omni / Veo Fast Video Prompts

## Usage standard

Each prompt uses the matching Banana Pro image as its **single reference image**. Upload the image to Flow and paste the prompt as natural language. The prompts contain no model-specific flags, so they stay on the common ground of Omni and Veo Fast. Default format: **8 seconds, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, one main action, no text and no logos**.

The general order from Google's Veo prompting guide is kept: **camera/shot → subject → action → context → style/ambience → audio**. Keep the number of actions limited in short clips. Do not redesign the source image's composition, the character's face, hair or clothing, the object's form or the environment's geometry. The audio line is kept separate; if you do not want generated music, combine the source song in the edit afterwards.

**Shared closing sentence:** `No dialogue, no singing, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no captions. Preserve the reference identity and composition. Vertical 9:16.`

## Quick application rule

After uploading the frame, keep the "preserve the reference identity and composition" sentence first. If the model breaks the character or object, do not add more decoration to the prompt; reduce the action count to one, slow the camera down and repeat "same face, same hair, same wardrobe, same environment geometry". If you do not want audio from the video model, keep the `no music` line and add your own music in the edit. On a first attempt, comparing the same prompt three times with small changes gives more reliable results than adding random new ideas.
