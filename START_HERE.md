# Start here (no API, no code)

You only need an AI chat assistant and any image/video generator.

1. Open [`01-graphic-dna/skill/SKILL.md`](01-graphic-dna/skill/SKILL.md), copy all of it into your AI chat.
2. Attach a reference video or image (or paste your lyrics) and write: "follow this".
3. You get a graphic DNA: palette, light, texture, motion + one locked character description.
4. Open [`02-motion-poster/skill/SKILL.md`](02-motion-poster/skill/SKILL.md), paste it into the same chat and write: "now make 20 poster prompts and 20 video prompts".
5. Paste the poster prompts into your image generator, then each poster + its video prompt into your video generator.

**Rule:** repeat the character description word for word in every prompt.

See a finished example: [`examples/echoes-led-head/`](examples/echoes-led-head/)

Automation (API, ffmpeg) is optional: see [README → Requirements](README.md#requirements-optional-automation-only).
