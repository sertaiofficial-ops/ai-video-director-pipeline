## Character constants in the reference set

The first reference is a premium hero poster: black/red cyberpunk night, red eye glow, chrome/organic mechanical transformation on one side of the face, black and glossy red jacket, metallic straps, chains and liquid chrome surfaces. The left side of the vertical composition holds a large vertical typographic area and technical markings; these must not be reproduced automatically in the generated clip, because the poster serves only as an aesthetic reference for the video.

The second and third references are character continuity sheets. The shared identity anchors are: androgynous/male read in their 20s with pale greenish skin, wet messy black hair, slim face, tired expression around the eyes, black leather jacket, black trousers, black boots, neon-green crack/corrosive vein pattern on the neck and face, small earrings and controlled, cold facial expression. The references cover front/back body, neutral face, smile, close-up and profile continuity.

## Implementation decision

The supplied Destiny Poison excerpt is used as the main audio; no separate AI music is generated. Instead of the full 13.077-second excerpt, the clip is designed as an 8-second section for the first test and mapped to 107.67 BPM with a rhythmic pulse of roughly 14 frames. Character identity and face/body continuity come from the references; the premium poster's typography and brand-like technical markings are not carried into the scene.
